About the workshop

What happens when AI mediates communication between people who differ in consequential ways?

People communicate across differences in opinions, knowledge, culture, abilities, roles, power, communication practices, and access to technology. AcrossAI treats these differences as central to the communication process: they shape what is said, how it is understood, and whose perspective prevails.

The workshop aims to bring together researchers from HCI, CSCW, communication, social science, and NLP to explore how AI shapes these interactions and develop a shared research agenda.

Three perspectives on difference

Difference as a condition
Differences shape the communication problem itself. Language differences may make translation central; expertise differences may call for collaborative sensemaking.
Difference as a challenge
Communication can lead to misunderstanding, conflict, stereotyping, or unequal influence. How does AI change these consequences?
Difference as a potential resource
Different perspectives can support learning, creativity, cooperation, and collective problem solving. When can AI help people benefit from them?

Workshop goals

Through cross-study discussions and collaborative synthesis, participants will examine design tensions, identify ways to evaluate individual, relational, and collective outcomes, and explore what generalizes across communication settings.

Planned outcomes include a white paper and an open evidence repository mapping the emerging AcrossAI research landscape and priorities for future work.
